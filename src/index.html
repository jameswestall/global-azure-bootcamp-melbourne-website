F.A.Q

  • Do I need to pay for a ticket?

    No! Global Azure is organised globally by individuals looking to give back to the Technology community. The event is completely free, sign up at meetup.com

  • How do I watch?

    The 2026 event is streaming live on YouTube! Head to our live stream on the day, or subscribe to our YouTube channel to get notified when we go live.

  • Can I present at the event?

    Our Call for Speakers is currently open! If you're interested in presenting, please submit your session on Sessionize. You can also reach out via info@melbourneazurebootcamp.com or Meetup.com. If you're a Melbournian looking to contribute to community events in the future, feel free to reach out as well!

  • Can my company sponsor this event?

    Yes! If your organisation is interested in sponsoring, please reach our to our organisers via info@melbourneazurebootcamp.com or Meetup.com

  • Will this be an in-person event?

    The 2026 event is fully online, streaming live on YouTube. We're already looking at returning to an in-person event for 2027 — stay tuned!
